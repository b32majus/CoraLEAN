## Healthcare improvement methodology

### SECA — Manual de Bolsillo Lean Healthcare

Official Spanish Lean Healthcare reference supplied for CoraLEAN's knowledge base.

Relevant because it provides healthcare-oriented Lean guidance and practical methodological material that can be cited when CoraLEAN explains or recommends Lean concepts/tools.

Official page:
https://calidadasistencial.es/manual-de-bolsillo-lean-healthcare/

CoraLEAN should curate/paraphrase the methodological knowledge and preserve source metadata rather than copy the manual wholesale. See ADR-004.

## Research literature

### Lean Six Sigma + ChatGPT

A 2026 action-research case was identified combining Lean Six Sigma, structured prompts, artefacts, human validation and stage gates. It is useful as a conceptual precedent but is not sufficient evidence of general effectiveness.

Reference:
https://eurekamag.com/research/110/233/110233390.php

### QI coaching evidence

A systematic review examined coaching/facilitation approaches in quality improvement. Evidence is heterogeneous and certainty is limited, so CoraLEAN should not make unsupported claims of effectiveness.

PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34981354/

## Research interpretation

The strongest converging insight is:

> AI is most useful here when the **method, workflow, state and governance sit around the model**, rather than asking a generic chatbot to "do Lean".

CoraLEAN therefore combines:

- healthcare improvement methodology;
- facilitation/capability transfer;
- structured workflow;
- persistent state;
- adaptive AI coaching;
- visual project orientation;
- source-backed methodological guidance with visible provenance when relevant.

No external product is being copied as a whole.
